# What You Need To Prepare

Before connecting real or simulated broker infrastructure, prepare:

1. Data source choice.
2. Broker simulation or paper account choice.
3. Exact commission, stamp tax, transfer fee, and slippage assumptions.
4. Initial paper-trading capital.
5. Allowed universe definition.
6. Maximum single-stock weight.
7. Maximum daily loss and maximum account drawdown.
8. Whether ETFs are allowed as a defensive asset.
9. Whether the system should require manual approval before every order.

Recommended first settings:

- Initial paper capital: 1,000,000 CNY.
- Max positions: 8.
- Max single stock: 12%.
- Max total equity exposure: 75%.
- Daily loss halt: 2.5%.
- Account drawdown halt: 10%.
- Paper trading duration: 60 trading days.
